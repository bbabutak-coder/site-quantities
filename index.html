Weight per metre = d² ÷ 162.2 (steel 7850 kg/m³). Add lap and hook allowances to the bar lengths. In No. of bars you can type a formula starting with =, such as =1/0.15 or =(4.2/0.15)+1. The result is rounded up to a whole bar. Bars are packed into 12 m lengths by cut length, longest first: a 6.8 m piece uses a whole bar unless a piece of 5.2 m or less can share the offcut. Offcuts count as waste.
