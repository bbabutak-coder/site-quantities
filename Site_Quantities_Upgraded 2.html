Weight per metre = d² ÷ 162.2 (steel 7850 kg/m³). Enter lap and hook/bend allowances separately for each bar; they are added to the base length before calculating total length, weight and stock bars. Allowances are manual inputs, not code-derived design values. In No. of bars you can type a formula starting with =, such as =1/0.15 or =(4.2/0.15)+1. The result is rounded up to a whole bar. Stock-bar planning uses a longest-first 12 m cutting-pack estimate and reuses compatible offcuts across bar marks of the same diameter. Offcuts count as waste. Any continuous bar longer than 12 m needs a separate splice/lap design check; this tool does not design splice locations.
